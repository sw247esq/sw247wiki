Aksum

Cultural, Economic · Apex Age: Antiquity

Starting bias Coast (dominant), Hill

Unique ability Kingdom of Natural Wealth

Antiquity
  • +3 Gold on Resources in Settlements adjacent to Coast.
  • +1 Trade Route limit with other civilizations, and all civilizations get +1 Trade Route limit with you.
  • Trade Routes to or from your Settlements have unlimited Range.
  • +30% Production toward constructing the Great Stele.
Exploration
  • +3 Gold on Resources in Settlements adjacent to Coast.
  • +1 Trade Route limit with other civilizations, and all civilizations get +1 Trade Route limit with you.
  • Trade Routes to or from your Settlements have unlimited Range.
Modern
  • +4 Gold on Resources in Settlements adjacent to Coast.
  • +1 Trade Route limit with other civilizations, and all civilizations get +1 Trade Route limit with you.
  • Trade Routes to or from your Settlements have unlimited Range.

Unique units

Dhow replaces Galley

25 / 20 / 20 combat · 3 moves · 2 sight · 50 Production, 2 Gold upkeep

+4 Combat Strength on Coast. 1 charge to found a Trade Route. Upgrades to Dhow II (replaces Quadrireme).

Tankwa replaces Trade Ship

2 moves · 3 sight · civilian, no upkeep

Its Trade Routes are immune to pillaging. Obtained automatically, not directly trainable.

Infrastructure & wonder

Hawelt improvement

40 Production (+20 per copy) · flat terrain only

+2 Gold flat. +1 Culture per adjacent Hawelt. +1 Culture per adjacent Wonder. Never removed at Age transition.

Great Stele wonder

275 Production · max 1 per player, 1 in the world

+200 Gold (scales with game speed) per Wonder completed in the city, including itself.
